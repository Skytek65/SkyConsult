The problem

When the furnace dies, the first form wins

Trade work is urgent and seasonal. A furnace fails on the coldest night of the year, a pipe bursts on a Sunday, a storm lifts shingles on a whole street. Homeowners search on a phone and contact two or three companies. The ones with a quote form get the request immediately. The ones with only a phone number get the callers who still have patience, and after hours, they get nothing.

Many contractor sites were built as online brochures. They list services and show a logo, but the quote request, the entire point, is missing or buried. Some send form entries to an inbox checked twice a week. During a cold snap, that delay hands the job to a competitor.

What I build

Built around the quote request

Your site puts a short quote form on every page: name, phone, town, type of job, and a few words about the problem. It is test submitted to your inbox before launch. Service pages cover what you do, and town pages cover where you do it, each with local SEO titles, descriptions, and schema, so you show up in the searches homeowners actually type.
