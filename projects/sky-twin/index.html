Sky Twin

Every day, find the place on Earth whose sky matches yours, then open a postcard from it.

Coming soon to the App Store Built with Codex Visit the product page

Screens from the Sky Twin product page.

Overview

Weather apps are about your sky. Sky Twin is about everyone else's. It's a small daily ritual that connects you with a faraway place that happens to share your weather right now.

Sky Twin reads your local conditions and compares them with fresh airport weather reports (METARs) from around the world. It finds today's twin: the place whose temperature, cloud, wind, precipitation, visibility and sun angle match yours most closely. You guess where it is on a map, then open a postcard written from its real weather, with a note about the town and a species recently recorded nearby. You can also call whether you'll still match at sunset and collect stamps in a passport.

The app is built as a ten-module Swift package. The modules cover NOAA solar geometry, a decoder for the worldwide METAR bulk feed, and a weighted similarity engine with deterministic rankings and hourly drift forecasts. There's also an open-data gazetteer (Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, GBIF) that keeps each record's license and attribution with the data. Postcards are written on-device with Apple's Foundation Models under a strict guard: every number in the generated text has to appear in the input facts, with a factual template as the fallback. On foldables, the hinge becomes the spine of a two-page atlas.

Key features

  • Daily twin: and Opposite Day, the place whose sky is least like yours
  • Guess the place: drop a pin on a map before the reveal
  • On-device postcards: written from real weather facts, with a hallucination guard
  • Twin Forecast: how long the match will last, plus a daily sunset call with streaks
  • Passport: a stamp for every revealed sky, collected by country and sky type
  • At a glance: widgets, StandBy and a Live Activity
  • Private: no account, no ads, no analytics
