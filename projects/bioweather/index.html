BioWeather

A health-first weather app that learns how the sky affects your body.

Live on the App Store Built with Antigravity View on the App Store

App Store screenshots, including the dual-screen layout.

Overview

Plenty of people feel the weather before they see it: a migraine as the pressure drops, aching joints ahead of a cold front. A normal weather app tells you it will rain. It doesn't tell you what the rain will mean for you.

BioWeather turns live weather, barometric pressure trends, air quality and pollen into daily risk scores for seven conditions: migraine, joint pain, sinus pressure, allergies, fatigue, mood and sleep. Log how you feel and it learns your personal triggers. It then shows them in insights, forecasts and proactive notifications, plus a report you can bring to your doctor.

All of the personalization runs on the iPhone. For each condition, a small ridge-regression model written in pure Swift maps weather features to symptom severity. It also tests lags of 0, 6, 12 and 24 hours to catch the “storm hit yesterday, I crashed today” pattern. Its predictions are blended with research-based scoring and weighted by confidence, so sparse or erratic logging can't wreck the forecast. Hard thresholds became smooth scoring curves, and every risk factor links to a built-in library of peer-reviewed research.

Key features

  • Seven risk forecasts: migraine, joint pain, sinus, allergies, fatigue, mood and sleep
  • Personal trigger learning: on-device regression with lag discovery and confidence blending
  • Forecasts: 10-day and 48-hour outlooks, with pressure-trend charts
  • Insights & research: correlation charts plus a cited research library
  • Doctor report: exports flare-ups correlated with atmospheric triggers
  • Everywhere on Apple: Apple Watch app, widgets, Live Activity, Control Center and dual-screen layouts
  • Private by design: no account; health data stays on the device
