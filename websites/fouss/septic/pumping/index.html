Pumping &
maintenance.

Keep your tank and leach field in good operating condition.

REGULAR CARE MATTERS

Don’t wait
for a backup.

Schedule by phone ↗

Septic pumping is necessary in every septic system. All systems must be pumped in order to reduce sludge buildup in the tank. For a 4-person household, tanks should be pumped every 3 years. A tank that is improperly maintained will need to be pumped more often. It is vital to pump the tank to maintain your tank and leach field in good operating condition. This will prevent costly repairs and replacement down the road. A pump job can usually be scheduled within a few days of your call. Emergency calls are also available if the tank is backing up.

With normal maintenance, a septic system can be relied upon to operate in an efficient and environmentally friendly manner for years to come.

BEFORE YOU CALL

Your pump-out
checklist.

When calling to schedule a pump job, there are a few things you should know:

  1. What kind of system do you have; Regular or Aerated?
  2. If your system is aerated, does the aerator (motor) work?
  3. When was the last time the tank was pumped?
  4. Are there lids on top of the ground? Or do they need dug up?
  5. How many lids are showing?
  6. If the lids need dug up, do you know where they are?
  7. Will you have the lids dug up or do you want us to dig?
  8. How old is the system? If you are not sure, how old is your home?
  9. How close can we get to the tank without getting out of your driveway? Approximate feet.
  10. If your septic system has an upflow filter, drywell, or other extra tank that may need pumped?
