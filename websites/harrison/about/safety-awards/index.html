Safety

Can you imagine feeling secure and having complete trust in the people working in your home?

Harrison Construction never takes for granted the trust you give us when you invite us to work in your home. Our safety record and clean work environment speak for themselves. We employ safe building practices in all the work we do. You can also rest assured knowing that our employees have successfully completed background checks, and that we enforce an active drug free workplace program.

Harrison Construction prides itself on having a safety program in place since 1998. We strive for perfection in all business aspects.
