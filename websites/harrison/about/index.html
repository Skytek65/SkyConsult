A company built on care.

Can you imagine your perfect home?

Harrison Construction, Inc. is the area’s most well-respected construction company. We can make your home or business exactly what you want it to be while maintaining a safe, professional work environment.

Can you imagine a construction company that thinks of the customer first?

Can you imagine a construction company that you can trust to do the job well? Can you imagine a construction company that shows up on time every day, cleans up at the end of each day and is staffed by reliable, responsible people? When Dan and Gillian Harrison started Harrison Construction in 1981, they imagined a company that made remodeling and building an exciting proposition rather than a series of unpleasant surprises, and this vision thrives today.

Harrison Construction is an established full-service remodeling contractor dedicated to quality work, a professional image and complete customer satisfaction. That means our customers are not only satisfied, but they are excited and anticipating starting their next project with us.

Having completed thousands of residential and commercial renovation, remodeling and repair projects since 1981, Harrison Construction has the experience and the know-how to complete your project better than you could have imagined.

Project Approach

Our personal commitment is to: understand your need, interpret your desire, research the feasibility and costs, develop the plan, and then execute the project. Our approach is to create a sustainable, quality improvement that justifies your financial investment as the homeowner, without surprises.

Our tools include: personal communication from start to finish, courteous and professionally trained work crews, as well as a respect for your home and your family.

Our approach leads to our goal of having you understand your investment, enjoy the construction process, and get more than you expected.
