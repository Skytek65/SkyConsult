First and foremost, the remodeled space should take into account the style of the home so it flows seamlessly with the rest of the house. Construction Process

Can you imagine a construction process you can count on? We consider remodeling or new construction a process, not a product. We strive to provide you with an enjoyable experience throughout the project, not just at its completion.

  1. 01

    Once contracted, we give a projected starting date that works out with our availability and your desired time frame. When we get to within a week of your job start we again update and confirm the actual starting day we will be there. We also project realistic completion dates.

  2. 02

    The first day of the job you will be introduced to the job site foreman. The foreman will be in charge of your project from beginning to end, and any and all communications can go through him to our office. In addition, the company supervisor will be in daily direct communication with the jobsite to monitor progress and attend to any situation which requires their assistance.

  3. 03

    If possible we prefer you be there on the starting day. We will take this opportunity to discuss household matters such as arrangements for parking, yard signs, home access, bathroom facilities (we typically bring a porta-john), family pets and any other special concerns. If arrangements cannot be made the first day we will meet at your convenience prior to the start of the job.

  4. 04

    Our work day is from 8 a.m. - 4.30 p.m. Monday through Friday, unless there is a special arrangement. Clean-up at the end of each day is an essential part of our work.

  5. 05

    Our foremen and crew wear company shirts and hats for identification and thus give comfort in knowing who is on your property and in your home. Respect for your home is of the utmost importance in our process. Professional etiquette in actions and language is standard company policy.

Clean Up

Can you imagine a construction company that fully cleans up its mess at the end of the day?

Harrison Construction serves as an unobtrusive and courteous partner in your home construction project. We know that your life doesn’t stop just because you are remodeling your home, so we clean up our mess at the end of every day and ensure that you can comfortably live in your space, even when construction is going on.

Our crew treats your home as if it is their own. It’s the little things that make the difference between a construction project being a positive experience or simply an inconvenience to the homeowner. We leave your home as functional as possible upon our departure each night, cleaning up and ensuring that you only see progress, not the mess associated with the process.

At Harrison, we take steps that our competitors overlook to ensure you experience unforgettable service and unparalleled support during the process. Below are just a few of the things we do to help make this a reality:

  • When working on kitchens, bathrooms and other high-traffic areas of your home, we work with you and your schedule to ensure that your space is functional when we leave each day
  • Daily installation of materials to protect your existing space
  • Cleanup of minor messes associated with the construction process as they occur
  • Daily updates and walk throughs with owners if possible
  • Daily removal of tools and additional materials
  • Coordination with homeowners on daily and overall construction schedule

Many homeowners state that they would never have noticed Harrison was in their homes, if it weren’t for the construction progress they saw each day. Experience the difference that courteous, focused workers provide. Call Harrison Construction today to learn how we can help with your next project.

Follow-Through

Can you imagine a comprehensive warranty on your entire project?

Harrison Construction stands behind its work. We offer a complete two year warranty on all of our construction work to support our number one goal-your satisfaction.

We provide you with a comprehensive 24 month warranty from the completion date of your project. This covers everything from re-caulking a sink to replacement of bad construction material and/or faulty installation. And if the fixtures and mechanical devices are purchased through our company or sister company The Material Difference, then our 2 year warranty covers those items also. No excuses and no expense to you.

Our goal is to leave you as completely satisfied 2 years later as you were on the day the project was completed. We understand that’s a strong statement and we are planning to follow through with it.
