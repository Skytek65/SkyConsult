Home / Process / Fee Structure

Clarity before construction.

Can you imagine having an accurate estimate before the project begins?

Harrison Construction provides free estimates for home owner projects. After a visit to your home, if detailed design drawings are needed to provide you with an accurate estimate, designs would be required. We can assist with the design process, or you can provide professional design plans obtained from others.

We strive to keep our fees affordable for our customers, but exceptional value does come at a price.

  • Insurance estimate fees range from $75.00 to $750.00, depending on the complexity of the project. This fee is fully refundable if Harrison Construction is hired for the project.
  • A consultation fee of $75.00 to $350.00 is charged for projects where the customer does not yet own the home. If you are the homeowner, there is no consultation fee.
  • If design plans & drawings are required, we will visit your site (without any cost commitment) and discuss the project to assess the involvement and follow up by sending you a design cost contract for your consideration, fees vary depending on the complexity of the project. This service is offered through our sister company, The Material Difference.

Typical payment schedules are as follows:

Fixtures and Custom Ordered Materials: If fixtures are ordered through The Material Difference Showroom or when a custom material item is ordered through Harrison Construction a 25-50% deposit for those item(s) will be collected upon ordering.

General Construction Materials and Labor:

  • 5% deposit of the total contract price will be collected with the signed contract.
  • 35% of contract total paid when physical work begins at the job site.
  • 35% of contract total at mid-point of job completion.
  • 15% of contract total near job completion.
  • Final payment of contract balance due within 10 days of job completion date.

We will also design the payment plans to fit individual needs and comfort levels.
