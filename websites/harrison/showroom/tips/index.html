Pre-Construction Tips

Wonderful design and construction don’t happen overnight. In order to make the process as easy as possible, we suggest you consider the following before the project begins:

  • Pack up the kitchen or bathroom in advance of the contractor’s arrival. Make sure you label the storage boxes to make it easy to find anything you may need. This will also make it easier to unpack.
  • Items that are fragile should be marked accordingly and stored in an area away from the construction.
