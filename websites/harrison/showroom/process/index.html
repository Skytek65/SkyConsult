Designed around you.

At The Material Difference, every customer and every project is treated individually. We understand that everyone's needs and desires are unique. All you have to do is let us know what you want our role to be, and that is what we will do. Whether you want a custom-created design you can complete yourself or you want us to coordinate the entire project, we will adapt to meet your needs.

In addition, should your project require construction, we can suggest the best contracting company we know—our sister company, Harrison Construction Inc.

Design & Installation Services
