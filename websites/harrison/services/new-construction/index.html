Build your vision from the ground up.

Can you imagine a construction company that takes Green solutions into account for all new construction?

Harrison Construction not only provides custom remodels and high quality construction repairs, but also assists in planning, design, and full-specification new construction on patios, additions, and exterior buildings. Harrison has built fully customized owner-spec homes, lavish outdoor living spaces with multiple onsite buildings and stone fireplaces, as well as additions to homes of all construction types. Harrison can meet any of your new construction needs.

Going Green

In addition to high quality, best practice construction procedures, Harrison stays highly attune to the Green market and its effects on new construction projects of any size. Green solutions are a major focus for many homeowners as they offer benefits in utility savings, material savings, as well as tax considerations. Let Harrison advise you on “Going Green” with your next project. Contact us for more details today.
