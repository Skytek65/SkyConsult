Green Techniques

Sustainable Solutions that Deliver Savings

Incorporating “Green” or sustainable techniques into your next home improvement project not only makes the carbon footprint of your home smaller, but it can translate into real utility savings as well.

Harrison Construction, Inc. is a full service remodeling contractor dedicated to developing sustainable solutions, while producing a quality work product. We adhere to a variety of Green de-construction and recycling practices and strive to provide a greater awareness of the natural environment through the construction industry.

Our company aims to have a positive impact on the environment through a number of green building techniques and products. Dan Harrison is a Green Certified Professional (GCP) through the National Association of the Remodeling Industry and has experience in making homes greener.

Harrison Construction offers assistance and consultation on a variety of different aspects of making homes more sustainable. Examples include the following:

  • Insulation Types and Styles
  • Windows and Fixtures
  • Efficient Appliance Choices
  • Renewable Home Improvement Products
  • HVAC Improvements
  • Recycle / Reclaim Resource Consultation

To learn more about Harrison Construction’s commitment to green construction techniques, visit the resources to the left or contact us directly at (740) 373-7000 or (304) 485-3773.

Green Remodeling

Can you imagine making an existing home or business cleaner, greener and more efficient?

What is Green? High performance, resource efficient, durable & sustainable, and healthy.

The older a building is, the more it will benefit from energy efficient changes. From windows and insulation, to high efficiency HVAC and appliances, the energy savings for green remodeling can make your home or business less expensive to maintain as well as more comfortable and better for the environment.

Whether you want to make changes for financial reasons, air quality, or environmental reasons. We believe that well-built is green built. Harrison Construction has the experience to make it happen.

Company Sustainability Statement

Harrison Construction, Inc. is a full service remodeling contractor dedicated to quality/sustainable work, complete customer satisfaction, and a commitment to de-construction/recycling practices.

We will share our knowledge of “green” building techniques and products by client education and through participatory community activity and volunteerism.

We will serve as a positive encouragement to the greater awareness of our natural environment, through the construction industry.
