Plumbing

Can you imagine worry-free plumbing in your bathroom?

We offer complete plumbing services for residential and commercial remodeling and repairs. For any plumbing needs ranging from leaking faucets to installing a new bathroom, our professionally trained plumbing staff is ready to assist you. Our plumbing work is performed in accordance with all local and state plumbing codes.

View the Harrison Construction Plumbing License

Electrical

Can you imagine feeling safe and secure with your electrical system?

We offer complete electrical services for residential and commercial remodeling and repairs with a professionally trained electrical staff. From repairing an outlet to rewiring a whole house, no job is too big or too small. All electrical work is performed in accordance with all local and state electrical codes.

View the Harrison Construction Electrical License

Mill Shop

Can you imagine custom woodwork made to your exact needs and specifications?

Our on-site Mill Shop offers services from making a window sill, fabricating custom base and casing trim work, duplicating cabinetry, building bookcase units, entertainment systems—even custom fencing. Our shop craftsman can service your every need. And, if custom design plans are required, our sister company The Material Difference, Inc. can help.

Design Services

Can you imagine your construction company and plan designer working together to create a beautiful space?

We offer complete Design Services through our sister company, The Material Difference. This includes room alterations, additions, whole house master plans, kitchens and bathrooms. Visit the showroom online to learn more!

If you decide to have the Material Difference create your designs and plans and Harrison Construction to complete your project, in addition to having a beautiful, professionally-done space, you have the added benefit of working with one point person on your entire project. With the Material Difference and Harrison Construction under the same roof, everyone is on the same page.

General Contractor

Can you imagine your project’s plans completed to perfection?

If your project plans have already been prepared, by others, but you are looking for someone to implement them, Harrison Construction can do that, too. Share your plans with us and we will be happy to provide you with an estimate. Whether our plans or yours, we will complete your project professionally and beautifully… it’s what we do best.

Maintenance

Can you imagine a construction company that cares enough to help maintain your house?

Do you need your deck stained or your gutters cleaned? Harrison Construction is primarily a general remodeling construction company, but we are also available to perform every kind of maintenance projects for our established customers.
