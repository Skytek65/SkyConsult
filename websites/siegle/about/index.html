About Siegle Law

Legal advice grounded in ethics, experience and knowledge, with a personal commitment to the people and communities Siegle Law serves.

Our mission

To provide quality legal services to the citizens of Pennsylvania and Maryland with competency, integrity and compassion.

Ethics, experience & knowledge.

Integrity, trustworthiness, experience and competence are of utmost importance when hiring an attorney. An attorney’s education and life experience, along with continuing legal education and interpersonal skills, enhance the attorney’s ability to serve your legal needs. A “life-long attorney” possesses the special ability to relate to a client while providing skilled and honest legal advice.

Our philosophy.

Attorney Siegle is committed to providing legal advice to her clients with integrity, competence and compassion.

Attorney Siegle appreciates and values the people and communities that she serves, which she demonstrates through her active participation, reduced-fee and pro-bono service to charitable organizations and projects.

Serving Pennsylvania & Maryland.

Siegle Law provides legal services to residents and businesses in Pennsylvania and Maryland. If you are a Pennsylvania or Maryland resident or business, or have a legal issue in the neighboring state, Attorney Siegle will provide competent legal advice.
