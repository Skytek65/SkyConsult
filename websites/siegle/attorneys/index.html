Linda S. Siegle, Esquire

A foundation of knowledge.
A commitment to people.

Attorney Linda Siegle is licensed in both Pennsylvania and Maryland. She is a graduate of the University of Baltimore – School of Law, and served as the law clerk for the Washington Suburban Sanitary Commission.

Attorney Siegle is a graduate of Johns Hopkins University, holding a Bachelor of Science Degree in Business and Management, and a Master of Science in Applied Behavioral Science (with a focus on Human Resources and Organizational Development). Attorney Siegle is a member of the Alpha Lambda National Honor Society through her achievements at Johns Hopkins University.

Attorney Siegle is a member of the Pennsylvania Bar Association, York County Bar Association, and Maryland Bar Association. She is a bonded title insurance agent for the Chicago Title Insurance Company and is a Certified Notary Public. She also serves as an arbitrator for the Court of Common Pleas of York County.

Attorney Siegle currently serves on the Board of Directors for the Hanover Area Chamber of Commerce and is a member of its Ambassador Committee. She also serves on the YWCA of Hanover Board of Directors as its President.

Attorney Siegle served on the Board of Directors for the Lake Meade Property Owners Association and Cedmont Community Improvement Association. In 2001, she was the recipient of the Carl W. Hobbs Community Service Award, an award given to the outstanding volunteer for service to the communities of Baltimore, Maryland. Attorney Siegle also served on the Church Council for Epiphany Lutheran Church located in Baltimore, Maryland.

Community roles and credentials are presented as described in the original Siegle Law website. Read more about community involvement.
