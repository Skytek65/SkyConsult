A collaborative process

Good work begins
with a conversation.

Dovetail assists our clients throughout the process, including the initial research of manufactured models, styles, and prices. We work with our clients to develop a design that reflects exactly what they desire. This is an important and necessary aspect of quality craftsmanship, and adds considerable value to the finished project, the flow of the construction process, and in giving clarity and detail to the homeowner’s desires.

Updates will be emailed so you can see the progress being made on your unit, or pictures can be brought to your home or office for review. This is a small expense, but it yields significant returns in the overall flow of the construction process, and in your satisfaction.
