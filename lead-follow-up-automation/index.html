The problem

The lead is rarely the problem. The wait is.

For most local businesses, leads are not scarce. Responses are slow. A form filled in at 8 in the evening gets answered at 10 the next morning, and by then the customer has booked someone else. It is not carelessness. The inquiry sits in an inbox, the owner is on a job, and the day fills up.

The trail is messy too. Requests arrive through the website, email, and social pages, and live wherever they landed. Some get answered twice, some never, and nobody can say which without digging. Speed, plus a single clean record of every lead, fixes most of it.

What I build

One workflow, built and tested in your account

I build one n8n workflow inside your own account. A new lead triggers three things at once: an immediate notification to you by email or Slack, a clean record in your CRM or Google Sheet, and, in the Standard package, an AI drafted follow up email built from a template you approve first. Nothing sends in your name until you have signed off on the words.
