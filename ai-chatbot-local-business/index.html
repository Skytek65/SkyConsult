The problem

Your customers ask questions at 9pm. Your website shrugs.

Most questions visitors ask are simple. What does it cost, when are you open, do you serve my town, how do I book. They arrive in the evening and on weekends, when nobody is there to answer. A static FAQ page answers some of them, if the visitor bothers to dig. Most do not dig. They leave, and the question becomes a lead for whoever answers first.

The obvious fix, a generic chatbot, creates a worse problem. Tools trained on the open internet will happily invent a price, a discount, or opening hours you never offered, and the customer holds you to it. A chatbot for a local business has to know less, and know it exactly.

What I build

Trained on your business. Tested before launch.

Your chatbot is built from your service pages, price lists, FAQs, and policy documents, and nothing else. Before it goes live it sits two tests. Twenty questions drawn from your own documents must be answered correctly. Five questions outside them must get an honest answer: I do not know, and here is how to reach us. Zero invented answers is the standard it ships against.
