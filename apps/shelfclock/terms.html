App license

Your use of Shelf Clock is governed by Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). These notes summarize key product information and do not replace that agreement.

A reminder tool

Shelf Clock helps you organize dates you enter or confirm. It does not determine food safety, provide medical advice, or guarantee a product’s condition. Check products before use, verify scanned dates, and follow product labels and manufacturer instructions. Reminders depend on device settings and operating-system behavior and are not guaranteed.

Free and Pro

Free includes up to 15 active items and core features. Optional Pro is offered at $2.99/month, $14.99/year with a 7-day free trial for eligible users, or $29.99 as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are in USD; local App Store pricing and availability may vary.

Subscriptions and purchases

Apple processes payments through your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. A yearly trial converts to the paid yearly subscription unless canceled before it ends; eligibility is determined by Apple. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account settings. Lifetime is a non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Restore Purchases is available in the app. Refund requests are handled by Apple.
