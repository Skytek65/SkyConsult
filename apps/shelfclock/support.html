Common questions

Notifications aren’t arriving

In iOS Settings → Notifications → Shelf Clock, allow notifications and check your alert options. Check Focus modes and Scheduled Summary, which may silence or delay alerts. In Shelf Clock, confirm the item date and reminder schedule. Open the app after making changes so it can refresh reminders. iOS limits pending local notifications, so the soonest reminders are scheduled first.

How do I restore purchases?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Open Shelf Clock’s settings or Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. You need an internet connection to check with Apple. If restoration fails, confirm the purchase is visible in your Apple purchase history and contact support with the error message, never your payment details.

Why isn’t iCloud syncing?

iCloud sync requires Pro. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud is enabled for Shelf Clock, and sync is enabled in the app. Check your available iCloud storage and internet connection. Open Shelf Clock on each device and allow time for Apple’s sync service. Keep a copy of important data before changing iCloud settings.

How can I improve scanning?

Use good, even light and hold the camera steady. Keep the printed date in focus and avoid glare or curved packaging where possible. Check the detected date and its order, especially for ambiguous day/month formats. You can always enter or correct a date manually. For PAO, scan text such as 6M or 12M and enter the opening date.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Shelf Clock, then choose Cancel Subscription or Cancel Free Trial. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Read Apple’s cancellation instructions.

How do I export my data?

CSV export is included in Pro. Open the app’s settings and choose Export CSV, then save or share the file using the iOS share sheet. Your exported file may include item names, dates and prices, so store and share it thoughtfully.

Shelf Clock is a reminder tool. Always check products before use and follow the label.
