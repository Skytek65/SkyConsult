More help, right this way ↗
Is Shelf Clock free?

Yes. Track up to 15 active items with scanning, core reminders and widgets for free. Pro adds unlimited items and the extras listed above.

Does it work for skincare and makeup?

Yes. Add a printed expiry date or a period-after-opening (PAO) label like 6M or 12M and the date you opened it. If you add both, Shelf Clock uses the earlier date.

Can it tell me whether something is safe to use?

Shelf Clock is a reminder tool. It cannot assess food safety, product condition or suitability for use. Check the product and follow its label and manufacturer’s instructions.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. Your shelf, scanning and local reminders work on your device. Optional Pro iCloud sync and Apple purchases use Apple services and need connectivity.

Will my items sync across devices?

With Pro, you can enable iCloud sync on devices using the same Apple Account. Your items stay in your private iCloud database.

What happens if I stop using Pro?

Your subscription features remain available through the paid period. Free includes up to 15 active items. You can manage your subscription in your Apple Account settings.
