Privacy, by design.

Your shelves are personal. They should stay that way.

The short version

Shelf Clock collects no data about you. We do not collect, receive, sell, share or track your personal information or the contents of your shelf. No account is required.

What stays on your device

Items, dates, locations, prices, notes, barcode-to-name history, preferences and usage outcomes are stored on your device. Shelf Clock contains no analytics, advertising, tracking technologies or third-party SDKs. The app makes no developer-operated network requests and performs no online product lookups. Apple services used for optional iCloud sync and purchases involve connections managed by Apple, as explained below.

Optional private iCloud storage

If you have Pro and enable iCloud sync, your data is also stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. The developer cannot access that private database. Apple provides and operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. Sync requires an internet connection and your Apple Account. You can disable sync in the app and manage iCloud storage in Apple’s settings.

Camera and scanning

Camera access is used only for on-device text and barcode recognition when you choose to scan. Camera images are not stored or transmitted. Only the item information you review and save, such as a detected date or barcode, becomes part of your local shelf data. You can revoke camera permission in iOS Settings and enter dates manually.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not send remote push notifications or receive notification activity. You control notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Payments, subscriptions and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Shelf Clock uses Apple’s StoreKit to determine your purchase entitlement on your device. Apple may process purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete items in the app. Pro includes CSV export so you can keep a copy of your data. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to backups you control. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete data in iCloud or cancel a subscription. Manage iCloud app data and device backups in Apple’s settings, and subscriptions in your Apple Account. We cannot retrieve or delete your private iCloud data for you.

Children

Shelf Clock does not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone else. It has no accounts, advertising or tracking. App Store age ratings and Apple’s parental controls govern downloads and purchases.

This website

This website uses no analytics, cookies, trackers, third-party scripts or externally loaded fonts. A hosting provider may process standard request information, such as an IP address, to deliver the website. Opening the files locally makes no hosting requests. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond; please avoid sending sensitive shelf data.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Material changes will also be communicated in the app where appropriate.
