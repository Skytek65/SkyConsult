A little help

Questions about your journal? We’re happy to hear from you.

Last updated October 19, 2026

Get in touch

Email skytekappledev@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Please leave out sensitive journal content. Support email is not an emergency service.

Photos and check-ins are optional

Choose “Write instead” to make a text entry. You can leave any before or after feeling blank and return to it later. There are no required daily entries.

Restore a purchase

Open the app’s Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. For a pending payment or billing issue, check your purchase status with Apple.

Manage a subscription

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and select Plate & Feel. Canceling stops future renewals; access continues through the paid period. Lifetime does not renew. Refund requests are handled by Apple through Report a Problem.

Keep a copy of your journal

Export and restore are included in Free. Use the app’s Settings to export an archive of your entries and photos. Keep your archive private. Before restoring, review the import preview.

About optional sync

Private iCloud sync is a Plus feature, off by default. Check that you’re signed into iCloud and have storage available. Local journaling remains available offline. If sync is interrupted, keep your local data while you contact support.

Make it comfortable

The app is designed for system text sizes, VoiceOver and appearance preferences. If something is difficult to read or use, tell us where you got stuck.

When things feel difficult

If you would like someone to talk to, Find A Helpline can help you find support resources in your country. This external service has its own privacy practices. Plate & Feel does not monitor journal entries or provide crisis response. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
