A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Wondering about
something?

We’re here to help ↗
Does it count calories?

No. Plate & Feel is calorie-free by design. It doesn’t analyze nutrition or assign food scores. It’s a place to remember and reflect.

Do I have to check in every time?

No. Save a photo or a note on its own. Skip any feeling or phase, or add it later. There’s nothing to complete.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. The free core works offline, indefinitely. Optional iCloud sync and purchases need Apple’s services.

Is this a health or treatment app?

Plate & Feel is for personal journaling and wellness only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or diet advice.

How do I restore Plus?

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used to buy Plus. You can manage subscriptions in Apple’s subscription settings.
