Using Plate & Feel

Plate & Feel is a personal food and mood journal for iPhone. Use it lawfully and only with content you have the right to store. Your entries belong to you. These terms describe the service alongside the Apple Standard End User License Agreement, which governs the app license.

Journaling, not medical care

The app is for wellness and personal reflection only. It does not offer medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, eating-disorder care or diet advice. Reflections summarize what you recorded; they do not establish causes or recommend foods. Do not use the app in place of professional care or emergency services.

Free and Plus

Free includes unlimited entries, check-ins, tags and search, current-week reflections, reminders, appearance choices, widgets, shortcuts, and full export and restore. Plus adds historical reflections, optional categorical charts, private iCloud sync and a lavender accent.

Reference prices in USD are $2.99 per month, $14.99 per year, or $34.99 for lifetime Plus. StoreKit displays the applicable localized price before purchase. No free trial. Lifetime is a non-consumable, one-time purchase with the same Plus features.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Apple charges your Apple Account after purchase confirmation. Monthly and yearly subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel through your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime does not renew.

Use Restore Purchases with the purchasing Apple Account to restore eligible access. Apple handles billing and refund requests under its policies and applicable law.

Your data when Plus ends

Your entries are preserved. Local journaling, access to existing entries, export and deletion remain free. New sync and premium reflection browsing require active Plus access. Keep a private export before changing devices or removing the app.

Availability and responsibility

Features may change as the app evolves. Device storage, backups, Apple services and network availability can affect access and sync. Keep backups of entries that matter to you. Nothing here limits rights or protections you have under applicable consumer law.

Privacy and contact
