PLATE & FEEL · PRIVACY, WITH ROOM TO BREATHE

Privacy, with room to breathe

Your journal is personal. Here is how Plate & Feel is designed to keep it that way.

Last updated October 19, 2026

Your moments stay with you

Photos, notes, tags and check-ins are stored locally on your device by default. No account is required. We do not operate a developer backend for journal content, receive your moments, sell personal data, or use advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.

Optional private iCloud

Plus offers sync through your Apple Account’s private iCloud using CloudKit. It is off by default. If enabled, moment information and photos are sent to Apple’s services under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We do not have access to your private journal database. Turning sync off preserves local moments and leaves existing private cloud copies in iCloud. To remove journal content across devices, delete all journal data while sync is enabled and allow your devices to sync before switching it off.

Photos and permissions

Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. Library selection happens when you choose an image. Imported copies have location and EXIF metadata removed; your original library images are unchanged. You can write without adding a photo. Optional reminders are local and off by default.

Reflections and purchases

Weekly reflections are calculated on your device from your recorded responses. No remote AI receives your content. Apple processes StoreKit purchases and supplies purchase status so the app can unlock Plus. We do not receive your payment card details.

Export, restore and deletion

Export and restore are free. Exports contain your journal text and photos and go only to a destination you choose. Keep exported archives somewhere private. You can edit or delete individual moments or delete all local data in Settings. Device backups and private iCloud copies are managed separately through Apple’s settings and the app’s sync controls.

This website and support email

This static website uses no cookies, analytics, tracking scripts, external fonts or embedded third-party content. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. Emailing support shares your email address and whatever you choose to include with us and the email providers involved. We use that correspondence to answer your request. Please avoid sending private journal photos or sensitive notes.

Questions or requests

Email skytekappledev@gmail.com about privacy or support correspondence deletion. We cannot access or delete moments held only on your device or in your private iCloud.

When things feel difficult

If you would like someone to talk to, Find A Helpline can help you find support resources in your country. This external service has its own privacy practices. Plate & Feel does not monitor journal moments or provide crisis response. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
