TRIALCATCH / PRIVACY

Your data stays yours.

Privacy policy · Effective October 18, 2026

No data collected. No analytics. No bank access.

What we collect

TrialCatch collects no personal data. There are no analytics, advertisements, trackers, third-party SDKs, or TrialCatch accounts. We do not sell or share your information.

Where your data lives

The trials, subscriptions, prices, dates, and notes you enter stay on your device. If you have Unlimited and enable optional iCloud sync, your entries are stored in your private iCloud database using Apple’s CloudKit. The developer cannot access that private database. iCloud is operated by Apple under its own privacy terms.

Network activity

The app makes no network requests to the developer or any third-party server. Optional iCloud sync and purchase processing use Apple’s system services. Opening a cancellation link takes you to the provider or Apple outside TrialCatch; those services have their own privacy practices.

Reminders and notifications

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from a server. TrialCatch requests notification permission so it can remind you of upcoming charges. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. Notification content may appear on your lock screen according to your iOS preview settings.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and payment information through the App Store. TrialCatch uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify your Unlimited entitlement. We do not receive your payment card or bank details.

No financial account access

TrialCatch never connects to your bank, accesses your cards, or reads financial transactions. You enter the details you choose to track.

Your choices

You can edit or delete entries in the app and turn optional iCloud sync off. Turning sync off does not by itself delete existing iCloud data. You can manage app data through your device and iCloud settings. Unlimited includes CSV export so you can keep a copy of your entries.

Children

TrialCatch does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address it.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the information you choose to send, including your email address, is used to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, card details, or other sensitive information. This correspondence is separate from the app, which collects no data.

This website

This static website uses no analytics, cookies, third-party fonts, or trackers. Your hosting provider may process standard request information to deliver the site under its own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We will publish changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will be explained here.
