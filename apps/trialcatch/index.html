try it.

More help this way ↗
Does TrialCatch cancel subscriptions for me?+

No. TrialCatch is a reminder tool. “Cancel now” opens the cancellation route and shows the steps; you complete cancellation with the provider. Always check for confirmation.

Is it really a one-time purchase?+

Yes. Unlimited is a $4.99 one-time purchase, with no recurring TrialCatch fee. You can also use Free for up to 3 active trials or subscriptions.

Do I have to connect my bank?+

No bank connection, card details, or TrialCatch account. You log the trials and subscriptions you want to track.

What if my iPhone is in Focus mode?+

Allow notifications for TrialCatch and enable Time Sensitive Notifications in iOS Settings. Focus settings can affect delivery. See Support for the setup steps.

Where is my data stored?+

On your device. With Unlimited, you can choose to sync using your private iCloud database. The developer cannot access it.

Can I track subscriptions I already pay for?+

Yes. Log an ongoing subscription to track renewal dates and costs. Trial reminders default to 3 days, 1 day, and 3 hours before; subscription renewal reminders are optional, 2 days before.
