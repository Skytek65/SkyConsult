Reminders aren’t arriving

  1. Open Settings → Notifications → TrialCatch and turn on Allow Notifications. Choose the alerts you want, including Lock Screen.
  2. Enable Time Sensitive Notifications for TrialCatch. In Settings → Focus, open the Focus you use and allow TrialCatch or time-sensitive notifications.
  3. Check the entry’s charge date, time, and reminder settings. Reminders whose scheduled time has already passed are skipped.
  4. Check your device’s date and time settings and update iOS when available.

iOS controls notification delivery. Focus and notification settings can affect alerts, so keep a separate reminder for a deadline you cannot miss.

Restore an Unlimited purchase

Use the same Apple Account you used to buy Unlimited. Open TrialCatch’s upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. An internet connection is needed for Apple to verify the purchase. If it doesn’t appear, check your App Store purchase history and contact support.

iCloud sync isn’t updating

iCloud sync requires Unlimited. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud Drive is enabled, and TrialCatch is allowed to use iCloud. Enable sync in TrialCatch, check available iCloud storage, and allow time for Apple’s service to update. Don’t delete your entries to troubleshoot sync.

Set up Siri & Shortcuts

Open Apple’s Shortcuts app, create a shortcut, and search the actions for TrialCatch. Choose “Log a free trial in TrialCatch,” set the service and trial length, then save. Use the shortcut’s name with Siri. Open TrialCatch to check that the entry and charge date are correct.

How to cancel an App Store subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Select the subscription and choose Cancel Subscription or Cancel Free Trial when available. Follow Apple’s current cancellation instructions. If the subscription isn’t listed, you may be billed directly by the provider; check your receipt.

TrialCatch doesn’t cancel for you

“Cancel now” opens the provider’s cancellation route with guidance. You must complete cancellation with the provider and check for confirmation. Marking an entry canceled in TrialCatch only updates your log. Steps can change; check with the provider.

Export a CSV

With Unlimited, open TrialCatch’s settings and choose Export CSV. Save the file using the iOS share sheet. The export contains the entries you’ve logged; keep it somewhere private.

Understand the savings total

“Saved by canceling” is an estimate based on entries you mark canceled. It counts the first paid period of a trial canceled before its charge date and annualized costs of canceled subscriptions. It is not a bank balance, a refund, or a guarantee of savings.
