Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select BioWeather Health and cancel your subscription. You can also manage it in App Store account settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid automatic renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID used for the original purchase, then reopen BioWeather Health. If your subscription is not recognized, email support for help restoring access. You do not need to purchase a second subscription.

What devices are supported?

BioWeather Health runs on iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later.

How does the free trial work?

All new subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Afterward, the selected plan is $0.99/month or $9.99/year in USD; prices may vary by region. The subscription automatically renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends. Manage or cancel in App Store account settings.

Where can I get help with an Apple charge?

Apple processes payments. Review your subscriptions in App Store account settings. For purchase issues and refund requests, visit Apple purchase support.

Is BioWeather a substitute for medical advice?

No. It is a wellness forecaster, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns.
