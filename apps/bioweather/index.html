07 / Good to know

A few clear answers.

Need a hand? Visit support ↗

What is biometeorology?

Biometeorology studies the relationship between atmospheric conditions and living things. BioWeather uses this research to translate weather data into a personal wellness forecast.

Do I need an account?

No. BioWeather requires no account, and all processing happens on your device.

How does the free trial work?

All new subscribers receive a 7-day free trial. After the trial, your selected subscription is $0.99/month or $9.99/year in USD. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid renewal. Manage your subscription in App Store account settings.

Which devices are supported?

BioWeather Health is available for iPhone and requires iOS 17.6 or later.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Your health data stays on your device. There is no tracking, cloud storage, sale of personal data, or third-party analytics.

Is BioWeather medical advice?

No. BioWeather is a wellness forecaster, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.
