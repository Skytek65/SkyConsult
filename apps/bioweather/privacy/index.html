BioWeather Health

Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Your health data is yours alone. BioWeather Health is developed by Skyler Martin and is designed to process your information on your device, without an account or a developer-operated cloud store.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how BioWeather Health handles information when you use the iPhone app, purchase a subscription, or contact support. Apple’s services and external websites have their own privacy policies.

2. Information handled on your device

BioWeather processes your health information, personal weather profile, and preferences on your device to provide its wellness forecasts and personalization. All app processing happens on-device. Your health data is not uploaded to us, and we do not maintain cloud storage for it. No account is required.

3. No tracking, analytics, or sale of data

BioWeather does not track you, use third-party analytics, or sell personal data. We do not use your health data for advertising or share it with data brokers. We do not maintain a server-side health profile about you.

4. Subscriptions and Apple

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple’s handling of App Store transactions and your Apple ID is separate from BioWeather’s on-device processing.

5. Support communications

If you email skytekappledev@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Email is separate from the app’s on-device storage and is handled by email service providers. Please do not send health records, payment details, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to address your request and any related follow-up or applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence by emailing the same address.

6. Your control and data retention

Your app data remains on your device rather than in a developer cloud account. You control your device and app permissions through iOS settings. Because we do not hold your on-device health data, we cannot access, retrieve, or delete it remotely. For questions about managing local app data, contact support. Apple manages subscription and transaction records under its own policies; removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

7. Security

Keeping health processing on-device limits the information sent to us. Protect your device and Apple ID to help keep your information secure. No method of storage or communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children’s privacy

BioWeather does not require accounts or collect children’s health data on developer servers. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through a support email, contact us so we can address it and delete the correspondence as appropriate.

9. Privacy requests

You may contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to support, including access, correction, or deletion. We will respond in accordance with applicable law. We cannot provide copies of health data that stays solely on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Review this page for the current policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Skyler Martin at skytekappledev@gmail.com.
