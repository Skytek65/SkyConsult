BioWeather Health

Subscription terms

Effective November 8, 2026

These terms explain subscriptions to BioWeather Health, developed by Skyler Martin. Review the plan and price displayed by Apple before confirming your purchase.

1. Download and requirements

BioWeather Health is free to download for iPhone and requires iOS 17.6 or later. No account is required to use the app.

2. Plans and free trial

All new subscribers receive a 7-day free trial. After the trial, the monthly plan costs $0.99/month or the yearly plan costs $9.99/year. The yearly plan saves over 15% compared with twelve monthly payments. Prices are in USD and may vary by region. Your App Store purchase confirmation shows the price applicable to you.

3. Payment and automatic renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. When a free trial applies, the selected paid subscription begins after the 7-day trial unless canceled in time. Your subscription automatically renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or current subscription period. Apple processes subscription payments through the App Store.

4. Managing or canceling a subscription

You can manage or cancel your subscription in App Store account settings. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select BioWeather Health. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to prevent renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

For billing questions or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support. Refund requests are handled by Apple under its applicable policies.

5. Health disclaimer

BioWeather Health is a wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Forecasts and scores are informational and should not be used to make medical decisions or replace professional care. Individual responses to weather can differ, and a forecast is not a guarantee of how you will feel. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.

6. Privacy

All app processing happens on-device. No account is required, and there is no tracking or cloud storage. See the privacy policy for information about app data, Apple payments, and support communications.
