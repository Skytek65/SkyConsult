Get your computer ready

Mac

Open System Settings › General › Sharing and turn on Screen Sharing (or Remote Management). In MdrnDuo, add the Mac with its name or IP address, choose VNC, and sign in with your Mac account name and password, or with a VNC password if you set one.

Windows

Turn on Remote Desktop in Settings › System › Remote Desktop (available on Windows Pro, Enterprise and Education). In MdrnDuo, choose Remote Desktop and sign in with a Windows account that is allowed to connect. On Windows Home, install a VNC server instead and choose VNC.

Linux and Raspberry Pi

Turn on a VNC server, for example with Raspberry Pi Configuration › Interfaces › VNC on Raspberry Pi OS, or a VNC server package on other distributions. Then add the computer in MdrnDuo with its address and VNC port (usually 5900).

Connecting away from home

MdrnDuo connects directly to the address you enter. To reach a computer outside your home or office network, use a VPN you already trust, such as Tailscale, and connect to the computer’s VPN address. Avoid exposing VNC directly to the internet.

Troubleshooting

MdrnDuo can’t find computers nearby

Nearby discovery is part of MdrnDuo Pro and needs Local Network access. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › MdrnDuo. Some networks, such as guest Wi-Fi, block device discovery. You can always add a computer by its address.

“Authentication rejected”

Check the username and password, and for Macs make sure your account is allowed in the Screen Sharing settings. For a VNC password, use the password configured on the VNC server.

Remote Desktop certificate warning

The first time you connect, compare the certificate fingerprint with the one on your PC before trusting it. If a pinned fingerprint later changes unexpectedly, don’t connect until you know why.

The connection is slow

Choose a lower quality in the computer’s connection settings, lower the remote screen resolution, or move closer to your Wi-Fi router.

Copy and paste

On VNC, MdrnDuo can send and receive plain text with Latin characters. Clipboard sharing for Remote Desktop isn’t available yet.

MdrnDuo Pro

Restore a purchase

Open MdrnDuo Pro in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › MdrnDuo on your iPhone and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Passwords stay in your iPhone’s Keychain, and MdrnDuo doesn’t send your data to me. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
