THE HELP DESK

A little help
with your diary.

Keep the stories. Untangle the rest.

Contact skytekappledev@gmail.com with your iOS version, Starstub version and a description of the issue. Please leave private entries out of your message.

Search finds nothing, or I’m offline.＋

Try a shorter title, the creator’s name, or a different media kind. Catalog availability varies by region and provider. You can always choose Add manually, including offline. Custom kinds use manual entry.

How do I import Letterboxd?＋

On letterboxd.com, go to Settings > Import & Export and export your data. Unzip the download and choose the diary.csv, ratings.csv or watched.csv file in Starstub Settings > Import & Export. Pro is required. Review the preview before confirming; existing matching titles are deduplicated.

How do I import Goodreads?＋

On Goodreads, open My Books > Import and export and export your library. Save the CSV to Files, then select it in Starstub Settings > Import & Export. Pro is required. Review the preview, dates and shelves before importing.

How do I back up or export?＋

Open Settings > Import & Export and choose CSV for a spreadsheet or JSON for a full diary backup. Save to Files or another destination you trust. Export is free. Keep dated copies before a major import or device change.

My iCloud diary isn’t syncing.＋

iCloud sync requires Pro. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud Drive is enabled, and Starstub sync is on. Check available iCloud storage and your connection, then allow time for changes to arrive. Export a backup before troubleshooting; avoid deleting data to force a sync.

How do I restore Pro?＋

Use Restore Purchases in Starstub’s Pro screen while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase. Check your internet connection. A subscription must still be active; a lifetime purchase can be restored without a subscription.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?＋

Open iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Starstub. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current billing period or trial ends to avoid renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. Apple’s subscription instructions explain the steps.

How do I add a widget?＋

With Pro, touch and hold your Home Screen, choose Edit > Add Widget and search for Starstub. For a lock-screen widget, customize your Lock Screen and choose Add Widgets. Set an entry to In progress and add page progress to populate Now reading. iOS controls refresh timing; open Starstub if the widget seems out of date.

Can I log with Siri or Shortcuts?＋

Look for Starstub actions in the Shortcuts app and add a quick-log action with a title, kind and optional rating. You can invoke your shortcut through Siri. Free entry limits still apply. Open Starstub to finish details or resolve an entry-limit prompt.
