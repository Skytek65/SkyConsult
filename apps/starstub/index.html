A FEW MARGIN NOTES

Good
questions.

Visit the help desk ↗
Do I need an account?＋

No. Open Starstub and start your diary. Your entries stay on your device, or optionally in your private iCloud with Pro.

Can I use it without internet?＋

Yes. Manual entry, ratings and reviews work offline. Catalog searches and new artwork need an internet connection; iCloud sync resumes when connected.

Where do search results come from?＋

Search results come from Apple, Open Library and Wikipedia, with release years from Wikidata. They supply catalog information and artwork; none of them endorses Starstub. Starstub shows which sources answered each search. Custom kinds are added manually.

Can I bring my old diary?＋

Pro imports Letterboxd and Goodreads CSV exports. Preview the import before adding entries. See Support for export instructions.

Can I take my data with me?＋

Always. Full CSV and JSON export is free, including when you no longer subscribe to Pro.

Is there a lifetime option?＋

Yes. Pro is $24.99 as a one-time purchase. Monthly is $1.99; yearly is $12.99 with a 7-day free trial for eligible subscribers. Only subscriptions auto-renew.
