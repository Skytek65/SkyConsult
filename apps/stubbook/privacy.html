YOUR DIARY IS YOURS

Privacy policy.

We make a diary. We don’t read it.

Effective October 19, 2026.

What the developer collects

The Stubbook developer collects no data through the app. There is no account, developer server, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK. Your entries, ratings, reviews, photos, and usage are not sent to the developer. This website also uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers.

Your diary & iCloud

Your diary is stored on your device. If you enable optional Pro iCloud sync, your entries are also stored in your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit, under your Apple Account. The developer cannot access that private database. iCloud use is subject to Apple’s terms and your iCloud settings. You can disable sync in Stubbook and manage iCloud data in Apple settings. Keep an export before deleting data or the app.

Searches & artwork

The only app network requests, apart from optional Apple iCloud sync and Apple purchase services, are catalog searches and artwork downloads sent directly from your device to Apple’s iTunes Search API and Open Library. Apple endpoints include itunes.apple.com and artwork on mzstatic.com; Open Library endpoints include openlibrary.org and covers.openlibrary.org. These providers receive search text, requested artwork identifiers, and ordinary network information such as your IP address. The developer does not receive these requests or operate a proxy.

Read Apple’s privacy policy and Internet Archive / Open Library’s privacy policy and terms. Search is optional. Manual entry works offline.

Photos & notifications

Custom cover photos are selected through the system photo picker. Stubbook receives only the photos you choose, without full photo-library permission. Selected covers stay with your diary and may sync if you enable iCloud. Optional reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push server is used. Change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and subscriptions through the App Store. Stubbook uses Apple’s purchase services to check Pro access. The developer does not receive payment card details. Apple handles its own transaction records under its privacy policy.

Imports, exports & your choices

CSV imports are processed on your device. Exports are created on your device and shared only through destinations you choose. Exporting, deleting entries, turning off iCloud sync, and deleting the app are your choices. Deleting the app does not cancel an App Store subscription or necessarily remove iCloud copies. Manage those separately in Apple settings.

Children

Stubbook does not knowingly collect personal information from children, just as it collects no app data from other users. Parents and guardians can use Apple’s parental controls to manage purchases and access to device features.

Support correspondence

If you email support, you choose what information to share. Your email address and message are used to answer your request; do not include private diary exports unless you intend to share them. Email is separate from the app’s data practices.

Changes

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date. Any new app data practices will be described before they take effect.
