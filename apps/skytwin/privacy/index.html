The desk / Privacy policy

Your skies,
on your device.

Effective October 24, 2026

Sky Twin has no account, analytics, ads or tracking. The developer runs no server for the app and receives no personal data from the app.

Location and weather

Location permission is when-in-use only. Your location is used on your device to read local weather and choose the sky to compare. Precise coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit for weather and to Apple Maps to find the place name. These services are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

You can use Sky Twin without location permission by choosing a city. You can change location permission at any time in iPhone Settings.

The app downloads the public worldwide METAR file from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center. That download is not specific to your location.

Place notes and nearby life

Requests to Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and GBIF concern the twin station's place, rather than your location. These services receive the requests directly, including ordinary network information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply: Wikimedia and GBIF.

What stays on your iPhone

Settings, saved cities, guesses, sunset calls, your Passport and cached weather are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes this local data. Widgets and Live Activities show data stored on your device; they do not send it to a developer server.

Postcards and Apple Intelligence

When available, on-device Apple Intelligence writes postcards from weather and place facts. No text leaves your device for that writing. When it is unavailable, a factual template provides the postcard.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases and subscriptions through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details. Sky Twin uses Apple's purchase status to unlock Plus. Apple's privacy policy and App Store terms apply to purchases.

Children

Sky Twin does not ask children or other users to create an account or submit personal information. If you have a concern about a child's use of the app, contact the developer.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Skyler Martin at skytekappledev@gmail.com. If you choose to email, the information you include is used to respond to your request.
