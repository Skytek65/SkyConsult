For questions or a problem with Sky Twin, email skytekappledev@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem appeared. Please omit precise location or other personal details unless needed to explain the issue.

Can I use it with location turned off?

Yes. Choose a city during setup, or open the desk drawer and select Change home city. You can return to current location later.

Why does my twin come from an airport?

Airports worldwide publish regular, standardized weather observations called METAR reports. Sky Twin compares recent reports with your weather. The airport is a reference point; conditions in the nearby city may differ.

When does the twin change?

Your daily twin is chosen once per local day. Plus also offers Reroll for another twin and Opposite Day for a contrasting sky.

How do sunset calls resolve?

Make a Yes or No call about whether your skies will still match at the next local sunset. The app resolves it when you open or return to Sky Twin after that sunset, using the Twin Forecast and latest observations. If the needed weather data is unavailable, the call is marked NO DATA.

How do I restore Plus?

Open Sky Twin Plus from the desk drawer, then choose Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage subscriptions in App Store account settings; Apple handles refund requests.

Why does a widget say “Open Sky Twin”?

Widgets need weather data shared by the app. Open Sky Twin and let today's sky finish loading, then check the widget again. Until data is shared, the widget asks you to open Sky Twin.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove its data stored on your device, including settings, guesses, calls, Passport and cached weather. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it separately in App Store account settings.
