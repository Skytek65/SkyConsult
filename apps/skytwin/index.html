01 / Follow the weather

A place you know.
A place to discover.

Temperature, clouds, wind and the light overhead can connect two places far apart. A new twin arrives once each local day.

  1. Read your sky

    Sky Twin reads your local weather. Use your location while the app is open, or choose a city.

  2. Find its twin

    It compares your sky with recent airport reports (METAR) worldwide to find today's twin. Drop a pin on the guess map before the reveal.

  3. Open a postcard

    Reveal the place and read a postcard written on your device, with weather, place notes and nearby life records.

02 / Inside the atlas

of passing skies.

Apple Intelligence writes from the supplied weather and place facts when available. A factual template supplies the postcard otherwise.
