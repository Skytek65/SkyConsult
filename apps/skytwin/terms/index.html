Sky Twin: Weather Postcards is licensed under Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. These notes describe purchases and content in the app.

Sky Twin Plus

The app is free to use. Optional Plus plans are $2.99 per month, $14.99 per year, or $34.99 for lifetime access. Prices are in USD; your App Store displays the applicable local price. Lifetime Plus is one payment and does not renew.

The yearly plan offers a 1-week free trial for eligible subscribers, then costs $14.99 per year. The App Store confirms eligibility and the price before purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation, or at the end of a free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in App Store account settings. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

Use Restore purchases in the Plus page to restore access with the same Apple Account. Refund requests are handled by Apple at Report a Problem.

Weather is for interest only

Sky Twin is for discovery and personal interest. Weather, forecasts, matching scores, sunset calls and postcards can be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate. Do not use them for aviation, navigation or safety decisions. Airport conditions can differ from nearby city conditions.
