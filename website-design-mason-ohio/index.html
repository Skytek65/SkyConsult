The problem

A site that cannot take a booking is a sign, not a salesperson

Most Mason businesses already have a website. The problem is what happens when someone finds it at 9 in the evening, ready to book. The site shows a phone number and opening hours, and nothing else. There is no booking button, no request form, and no way to leave a name and a need. So the visitor does the obvious thing and taps the next result, where booking takes thirty seconds.

Older sites make it worse. Pages built for a desktop screen load slowly on a phone and push the phone number below the fold. Forms, where they exist at all, often send to an inbox nobody checks daily. Every one of those moments is a customer who chose you first and still ended up elsewhere.

What I build

A site built to take the booking
